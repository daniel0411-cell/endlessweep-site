How to read your Minesweeper statistics

Win rate

Win rate is wins divided by games played. A lower rate is normal when you move to a harder board or test riskier strategies. Compare the same mode over a meaningful run of games instead of judging one session.

Best time

A best time is useful only when the board size, mine count, first-click rule, input method, and timing method are comparable. Beginner, Intermediate, Expert, Daily, Zen, and Custom results are kept separate here. Use the board-size guide before comparing difficulties.

Recent trends

The trend cards summarize your latest 10 completed games. Recent win rate shows consistency, average time shows pace, and efficiency shows how directly your actions advanced the board. A slower win can still be a better result if it replaces guesses with verified moves.

Endlessweep Efficiency is not 3BV/s

Endlessweep Efficiency is the percentage of recorded reveal, flag, unflag, and chord actions that advanced the game. It is a lightweight local feedback metric capped at 100%. Standard 3BV/s uses a separate board-complexity calculation and cannot be derived from this percentage, so the two values should not be compared.

Use Replay to improve

Open a saved replay and inspect unnecessary flags, repeated actions, missed chords, and the move before a loss. The latest 10 full replays are retained in this browser; older history may remain without replay data. Practice recurring mistakes in the interactive training center.

Local data and privacy

Aggregate results, recent history, Daily streaks, and replays use browser local storage. Clearing site data, using private browsing, or switching devices removes or separates this history. The Clear button deletes both local statistics and local replays.
