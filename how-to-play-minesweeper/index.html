How to Play Minesweeper

Learn the rules and controls, then start a free game.

The goal

Clear every safe square without opening a mine. The numbers show how many mines touch a square, including the eight squares around it.

How the numbers work

  • A 1 means one neighboring square contains a mine.
  • A 2 means two neighboring squares contain mines.
  • A blank square has no neighboring mines and usually opens a larger safe area.

Reveal and flag squares

Click a hidden square to reveal it. When you know where a mine is, flag it so you do not open it by mistake. On desktop, use right-click to place or remove a flag. On mobile, switch to Flag mode before tapping.

A flag is a note, not proof. You can remove it later if your logic changes.

Difficulty levels

Start with Beginner to learn the board, move to Intermediate for a larger challenge, and use Expert when you are comfortable reading several clues at once.

Simple first move

The first reveal starts a new board and is protected from an immediate mine. After that, use the numbers to decide which squares are safe and which should be flagged.
