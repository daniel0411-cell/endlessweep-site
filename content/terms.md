## Use of the site

Endlessweep provides browser games, guides, and related tools for personal, lawful use. Do not attempt to disrupt the service, bypass network controls, abuse analytics endpoints, or misrepresent challenge results.

## Game results and challenge links

Scores, records, replays, and Daily history are generally local to the browser. A shared challenge link contains the information needed to recreate the selected board, but it does not prove that a result was achieved by a particular person.

## Content and availability

Guides are educational explanations and may need revision when game behavior or browser support changes. Features can be changed, paused, or removed as the project develops.
