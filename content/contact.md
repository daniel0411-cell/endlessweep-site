## What to include in a report

For a gameplay problem, include the mode, difficulty, device or browser, the steps that reproduce it, and what you expected to happen. A challenge link or screenshot can help when it does not reveal private information.

## Content corrections

Tell us which page or section is inaccurate and include the rule, board position, or observed behavior that supports the correction. We review reports against the current game behavior before changing a guide.

## Email

Email **contact@endlessweep.com** for site feedback and correction requests. Do not send passwords, payment details, private analytics exports, or other sensitive information.
