## Local game data

Game records, replays, Daily history, settings, and theme preferences are stored in local browser storage unless a feature says otherwise. Clearing browser data or switching devices can remove those records.

## Anonymous gameplay events

When enabled, the site may send limited gameplay events such as a mode name, result category, time, score, or cleared count to improve reliability and understand feature usage. Events are validated by the site worker and do not include the board layout, account credentials, or the contents of your local records.

## Contact requests

If you email us, we use the information you provide to respond to the request and investigate the reported issue. Do not include sensitive information that is not needed for support.

## Cookies and third-party content

The site may use essential browser storage and, if advertising or embedded media is enabled, third-party technologies may set their own cookies or similar identifiers. Browser settings can limit these technologies. See the [Terms of Service](/terms/) for the general use rules.
